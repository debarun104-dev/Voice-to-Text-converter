## 🚀 Project — Voice to Text converter

I've developed a simple voice to text converter. 
It takes in speech through your microphone and converts it to text. 

### 🎯 The Problem: Many of us do not have the time to type out long text or paragraphs and we would always wish for a system that could do the same for us.

### 💡Solution:- 
* 🤖 Here's where the voice to text converter drops in.
* 📚 Just speak into the microphone whatever is in your mind.
* 🌐 The system converts the speech to text.
* Then just copy paste the text wherever desired.
